- Repair is limited to explicit setup/upgrade/recovery-capable flows and must be followed by exact re-read/re-verification before route startup may continue.

- Reproduce the 2026-09-19 disabled-only forensic shape and retain negative tamper coverage.

- Do not make the generic verifier silently accept `enabled = false` as healthy; that would violate CIH-001/D29.
- Do not implement a broad "overwrite changed hook" recovery path; the compatibility exception is only the native enablement override on an otherwise exact hook.
