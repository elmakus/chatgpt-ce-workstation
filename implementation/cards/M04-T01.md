### Must preserve

- `chatgpt_only` continues to define normal ChatGPT as the fixed Task Card executor.
- Delegated workers are subordinate execution resources, not Card owners or workflow-state authorities.
- Worker execution is opt-in through a stable Card/profile contract; existing Cards without delegation preserve current behavior.
- Main execution awaits worker completion; normal periodic polling is not part of the contract.
- Raw worker transcripts/event streams stay outside main context by default; parent-facing results are bounded/normalized.
- Delegated tester input is task/review authority plus actual repository/worktree state, not executor transcript by default.
- Delegated tester verification does not satisfy REQUIRED/RECOMMENDED workflow Independent Review. Under `chatgpt_only`, that review remains a fresh normal ChatGPT chat.
- Delegated workers are leaf workers by default; no nested fan-out semantics are introduced.
- Workflow authority stays backend-neutral. Do not mention Muse argv, workstation paths or a `muse-worker` command in generic workflow rules.

### Must not / rationale that must travel

- Do not change `execution_policy`.
- Do not introduce worker selection as a Capability Gate or speculative preflight.
- Do not create a second mutable worker-state authority beside Task Board.
- Do not require every Card to delegate.
- Do not turn full worker logs into mandatory Task Board/evidence payloads.
- Do not weaken current independent-review freshness or human/live authorization gates.

## Outcome

Current workflow `main` gains a generic, policy-specific delegated-worker contract that Execution Prep and Execution can apply when a Card explicitly opts in, while all existing `chatgpt_only` ownership/state/review semantics remain intact.

## Acceptance

- Execution Prep preserves delegated-worker requirements in the stable Card contract rather than mutable Task Board state.
- Execution deterministically invokes/awaits a declared worker and consumes only its bounded normalized result on the normal path.
- Delegated worker failure maps to normal Card execution/blocker/corrective semantics without silently advancing the Card.
- No-poll and transcript-isolation semantics are explicit.
- Tester-vs-independent-review distinction is explicit.
- Existing no-delegation behavior remains unchanged.
- Generic workflow text contains no Muse-specific CLI/runtime details.
- Cross-file semantic audit finds no contradictory executor, review or state-ownership semantics.
