### Must preserve

- Codex Main remains orchestration, integration, acceptance, Project Workflow state, and escalation authority.
- Under active `muse-max`, Companion is one persistent internal Codex GPT-5.6 Luna XHigh worker and must never be launched through `runtime/muse_worker.py`.
- Exactly the six existing non-Companion roles are Muse-routed: `micro_executor`, `default_executor`, `senior_executor`, `tester`, `investigator`, and `archivist`.
- Every Muse assignment is a bounded fresh one-shot invocation; no nested Muse workers, no direct Muse sibling messaging, and no hidden conversational continuity between invocations.
- Executor and Tester are always separate invocations. Tester receives accepted verification authority, actual resulting workspace/repository state, and relevant required evidence — not the executor raw transcript/trajectory.
- Ordinary in-scope RED routes through Main: focused Tester findings -> fresh owning Executor repair capsule -> fresh Tester recheck. Cross-boundary findings stop at Main rather than expanding worker authority.
- Later capsules and Main normal context may use compact normalized results and bounded durable references, but must not copy raw Muse JSONL/stderr trajectories.
- Preserve M07 adapter failure/cancellation/log-isolation semantics and the unchanged behavior/allocation of `plus`, `luna-xhigh`, and `pro-x5`.
- The current Project Workflow execution policy is serial; M08 must not add or emulate lane concurrency.

### Must not / rationale that must travel

- Do not add a new generic scheduler/orchestrator or duplicate Muse role tree merely to run this pilot. Current D21/delegation semantics already define Main-owned sequencing; M08 verifies them.
- Do not use unmanaged background shell jobs to imitate future M09 concurrency.
- Do not treat worker self-report as acceptance without independent state/evidence verification.
- Do not intentionally pass executor raw logs, prompt transcript, or raw Muse trajectory to Tester or repair workers.
- Do not turn an ordinary controlled RED into a strategic escalation. Escalate only if evidence crosses scope, contract, ownership, architecture, security, migration, or authority boundaries.
- Do not rebuild/recreate the workstation, perform login changes, or make unrelated persistent runtime changes under this Card.
